Privacy Policy

How NexTrade Prime collects, uses and protects your personal data — and the rights you have over it.

1. Data we collect

We collect the information you provide to us and information generated by your use of our services. This includes account details such as your name, email address and contact details; information you provide when you apply for an account or contact us; trading activity, including your orders, positions and account history; and technical information such as cookies and usage data from your visits to this website.

2. How we use it

We use your data to open and manage your account, to provide and operate our trading services, to verify your identity, to process deposits and withdrawals, to respond to your questions, to meet our legal and regulatory obligations, and to improve our website and services. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Sharing

We share your data only where necessary: with service providers who help us operate our services, such as platform and payment providers; with our professional advisers; and with regulators, law enforcement or other authorities where we are required to do so by law, or where it is necessary to prevent fraud or other unlawful activity. Anyone we share data with is required to handle it appropriately.

4. Retention

We keep your data for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, and for as long as we are required to keep it by law. When data is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised.

5. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, to ask us to delete it where we no longer need it, and to object to or restrict how we use it, subject to our legal obligations. To exercise these rights, contact us through the contact form.

6. Cookies

This website uses cookies to remember your preferences, to understand how the site is used, and to keep the site working properly. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but some parts of the site may not work correctly if you disable them.

7. Contact for data requests

For any question about this policy, or to make a data request, contact us through the contact form. Tell us what you are asking for so we can respond to your request.

Risk Warning

CFDs are complex instruments and come with a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. You should consider whether you understand how CFDs work and whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money. Trading foreign exchange, commodities, indices and cryptocurrencies on margin carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose.
