How CFDs Work

When you trade with NexTrade Prime, you trade contracts for difference (CFDs). A CFD lets you trade on the price movement of an instrument — a currency pair, gold, an index, a cryptocurrency, oil or a share — without owning the underlying asset. You can open a long position if you expect the price to rise, or a short position if you expect it to fall.

CFDs are traded on margin, which means you put down a fraction of the full position value. Leverage of up to 1:500 is available, and leverage magnifies both gains and losses: a small price move can produce a large result relative to the margin you committed, in either direction. That is why position sizing, stop orders and a clear risk plan matter on every trade.

CFDs are complex instruments and come with a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. Make sure you understand how CFDs work and whether you can afford the risk before you trade. Read the Risk Disclosure before opening an account.
