Anti-Money Laundering Policy

NexTrade Prime does not tolerate money laundering or the financing of terrorism. This policy explains the checks we carry out and what we expect from our clients.

1. Our commitment

NexTrade Prime is committed to preventing its services from being used for money laundering, terrorist financing, fraud or any other unlawful activity. We apply customer due diligence, monitor transactions, and report suspicious activity as required by law.

2. KYC and identity verification

Before you can trade, we verify your identity. You will be asked to provide identification documents and proof of address, and we may ask for further documents at any time. We may use third-party services to check the information you provide. Accounts cannot be opened or operated under a false name, or on behalf of another person without our agreement.

3. Source of funds checks

We may ask you to explain the source of the funds you deposit, and to provide documents that support your explanation. This is a normal part of our obligations and may be required before a deposit or withdrawal is processed, or at any time while your account is open.

4. Monitoring

We monitor accounts and transactions on an ongoing basis. Unusual activity — including activity that is inconsistent with what we know about a client — may be reviewed, and we may ask for further information or documents.

5. Reporting obligations

Where we suspect money laundering, terrorist financing or other unlawful activity, we are obliged to report it to the relevant authorities. We may not be able to tell you that a report has been made.

6. No third-party deposits or withdrawals

We accept deposits only from accounts in your own name, and we send withdrawals only to accounts in your own name. Deposits from third parties will be returned, and withdrawal requests to third parties will be refused.

7. Cooperation with authorities

We cooperate with regulators, law enforcement and other competent authorities as required by law, including by providing information and records relating to our clients and their transactions.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us through the contact form.

Risk Warning

CFDs are complex instruments and come with a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. You should consider whether you understand how CFDs work and whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money. Trading foreign exchange, commodities, indices and cryptocurrencies on margin carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose.
