Trade Gold & Metals

Trade gold and silver, the world’s most watched metals. XAUUSD and XAGUSD let you trade on the price of gold and silver without owning any physical metal.

Gold and Silver Trading

Gold (XAUUSD) and silver (XAGUSD) are quoted in US dollars per troy ounce. You trade CFDs on their price, so you can open a long position if you expect the price to rise or a short position if you expect it to fall, without buying, storing or insuring any physical metal. Gold is the most actively traded metal, and silver tends to move more sharply than gold, in both directions.

Why Traders Trade Gold

Traders follow gold for several reasons. It is often described as a safe haven, because demand for gold has often risen in times of market stress or geopolitical tension. It is also widely seen as an inflation hedge, because gold is a real asset whose supply cannot be printed. And because gold is priced in US dollars, it often moves in the opposite direction to the dollar. This is market commentary, not advice: none of these patterns is guaranteed, and gold can fall steeply even when uncertainty is high.

What Moves Gold Prices?

Gold prices respond to the US dollar, to interest rates, to inflation expectations and to demand for safety in uncertain times. Higher real interest rates tend to weigh on gold, because gold pays no interest; a weaker dollar tends to support it. Central bank buying, jewellery demand and mining supply also play a part over longer periods.

Trading Hours

Gold and silver trade almost around the clock on weekdays, from Sunday evening to Friday evening (GMT), with a short daily break. Liquidity is usually highest during the London and New York sessions.

Risks to Know

Gold can move quickly, and silver moves faster still. Leverage of up to 1:500 magnifies both gains and losses, and prices can gap around news. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose, and read the Risk Disclosure before you trade.
