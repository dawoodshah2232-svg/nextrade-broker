MetaTrader 5

MetaTrader 5 (MT5) is the industry-standard trading terminal, used by traders around the world for forex, metals, indices and more. It combines powerful charting, automated trading and market depth in one workspace, on desktop, web and mobile.

Key features

  • Advanced charting with 21 timeframes and 80+ built-in technical indicators
  • Expert Advisors (EAs) for automated trading strategies
  • Depth of Market and one-click trading from the chart
  • Built-in economic calendar and market news
  • Available on desktop, web and mobile with a single login

Who it suits

Traders who want a complete, full-featured terminal, and anyone interested in automated strategies or in-depth technical analysis. If you have traded before, MT5 will feel familiar from day one.

cTrader

cTrader is a modern trading platform built for speed and clarity. Its depth of market view shows the available liquidity at each price, and one-click trading lets you act on a move the moment you see it.

Key features

  • Depth of Market (Level II pricing) showing available liquidity at each price level
  • One-click trading and quick-trade buttons for fast order entry
  • Fast execution with advanced order types, including stop and limit orders
  • Detachable charts and a clean, customisable workspace
  • cTrader Automate for algorithmic trading

Who it suits

Day traders and scalpers who value execution speed, transparent pricing and a clear view of the order book. It also suits traders who want a modern interface without giving up professional tools.
