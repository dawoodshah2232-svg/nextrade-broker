Commissions & Swaps

Commissions

Raw accounts pay $3.50 per lot, per side. Pro accounts pay $2.50 per lot, per side. Standard accounts pay $0 commission — the cost is included in the spread.

Swaps

Positions held overnight may incur a swap charge or credit, depending on the instrument and the direction of your trade. Swap-free accounts are available on request — contact support to ask about the options on your account.

Leverage & Margin

Leverage up to 1:500 is available on all account types — Standard, Raw and Pro.

ExampleLeverageMargin required
1 lot EURUSD1:500About $200

Example only: 1 lot of EURUSD at 1:500 leverage requires about $200 in margin. The exact margin depends on the instrument price at the time you open the trade.

Higher leverage increases both potential gains and potential losses. A small market move can have a large effect on a leveraged position, so use leverage carefully and only trade with money you can afford to lose. Read our risk disclosure before you trade.
