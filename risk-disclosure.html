Risk Disclosure

Please read this notice carefully before you trade. It explains the risks of trading CFDs and foreign exchange on margin.

1. CFDs are leveraged products

Contracts for difference (CFDs) and foreign exchange are leveraged products. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses, and a small market movement can have a large effect on the value of your position — in either direction.

2. Most retail accounts lose money

Most retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs. You should consider whether you understand how CFDs work and whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money.

3. You can lose more than your deposit

Where negative balance protection does not apply, you can lose more than the money you deposited. This can happen when markets move sharply, for example after a news event or when a market gaps, and a position cannot be closed at the price you expected. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

4. Volatility in forex and crypto

Foreign exchange and cryptocurrency markets can be highly volatile. Prices can move rapidly and unpredictably, spreads can widen, and liquidity can fall — especially around economic announcements, during low-liquidity hours, and in times of market stress. Cryptocurrencies can be particularly volatile.

5. No investment advice

Nothing on this website is investment advice, and nothing on this website should be taken as a recommendation to buy or sell any financial instrument. NexTrade Prime does not provide investment advice. Any decision to trade is yours alone, and you should seek independent advice if you are unsure whether trading is suitable for you.

6. Past performance

Past performance is not indicative of future results. Historical prices, examples and educational material on this website are not a promise or a forecast of future performance.

7. Other risks

Trading carries additional risks, including the risk of technical failure, interruptions to trading platforms or internet connections, and changes in market conditions that affect execution. Stop-loss orders do not guarantee that a position will be closed at the price you set — in fast-moving markets a position may be closed at a worse price. You are responsible for monitoring your open positions and maintaining enough margin in your account.

Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose. If you do not understand the risks described here, do not trade.
