About NexTrade Prime

A forex broker built around raw spreads, fast execution and honest pricing — for traders who want to know exactly what they are paying.

Built for traders, not for noise

NexTrade Prime is a forex broker offering trading in forex, gold, indices, cryptocurrencies, energies and shares through CFDs, on MetaTrader 5, cTrader and TradingView. We focus on the things that matter on every trade: the spread you pay, the speed your order is filled, and the clarity of the price you see.

Our accounts are simple to compare. Standard, Raw and Pro differ in minimum deposit, typical spreads and commission — nothing else is hidden in the fine print. Spreads shown on this site are typical spreads from the stated level and vary with market conditions, and leverage of up to 1:500 is available on eligible accounts.
