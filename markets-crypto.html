Trade Cryptocurrencies

Trade Bitcoin and Ethereum around the clock. Crypto CFDs let you trade on price movements 24/7, without owning any coins or managing a wallet.

Bitcoin and Ethereum CFDs

BTCUSD and ETHUSD are the two most traded cryptocurrency pairs. With a CFD you trade on the price of Bitcoin or Ethereum against the US dollar, without owning any coins, without a wallet and without holding assets on an exchange. You can open a long position if you expect the price to rise, or a short position if you expect it to fall.

Crypto is highly volatile. Bitcoin and Ethereum can move by large amounts in a single day, in either direction, and sharp falls are common. Trade crypto only with money you can afford to lose, size positions carefully, and never assume a price will recover.

24/7 Trading

Unlike forex or shares, cryptocurrency markets never close. You can trade BTCUSD and ETHUSD 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. That also means prices can move sharply while other markets are closed, so a position held over the weekend is still exposed to price moves.

What Moves Crypto Prices?

Crypto prices move on demand for the coins themselves, on news about regulation, on the launch of new products such as exchange-traded funds, and on the broader mood of risk markets. A single announcement can move Bitcoin by thousands of dollars in hours, and smaller coins move more than Bitcoin.

Risks to Know

Crypto is the most volatile market on this site. Leverage of up to 1:500 magnifies both gains and losses, and a leveraged crypto position can lose its full margin quickly. There is no circuit breaker and no closing bell to stop a fall. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose, and read the Risk Disclosure before you trade.
